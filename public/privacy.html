Privacy Policy — UPlanet Ẑen

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Coracle is a client for the NOSTR protocol, adapted for the UPlanet cooperative ecosystem. Unlike a typical centralized service, there is no single company collecting your data: it is hosted by the Astroport.ONE station you choose (your "home station"), operated by its "Captain".

What we collect

  • Your NOSTR public key (MULTIPASS) and the profile information you choose to publish (name, avatar, banner, etc. — kind 0 event).
  • Content you publish (notes, reactions, messages) — public by default on the NOSTR protocol, except when encrypted (private messages).
  • Standard technical connection logs (IP address, timestamps) kept by the operator of the relay or station you use, for security and service operation purposes.
  • If you set up a Ẑen/Ğ1 wallet, associated transactions are public on the Ğ1 blockchain (Duniter) — an inherent property of this free currency, independent of Coracle.

File storage (uDRIVE)

Files you upload (images, videos, documents) are stored on IPFS, a content-addressed storage network. An unencrypted file is potentially accessible to anyone who knows its CID. Images attached to a private message can be end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before being uploaded to IPFS; the decryption key is then only shared inside the encrypted message sent to your recipient.

The BRO assistant

When you interact with the "BRO" AI assistant, the content of your request (text or image) is processed on the cooperative's own machines to generate a response. This processing is used for no other purpose: no profiling, no reselling to third parties, no targeted advertising.

Cookies

Coracle uses a minimal set of technical cookies, necessary for your session (interface preferences, language, theme). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers for commercial purposes.

Deleting your data

You can request the deletion of published content (NIP-09). This request is broadcast to the network's relays, but we cannot guarantee that all of them will honor it: the NOSTR protocol is decentralized, and some relays or stations may retain copies already replicated. You alone hold your private key: no one else can decide to "delete" it on your behalf.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and portability over personal data you have shared with us directly (for example by email). For data published on the NOSTR protocol itself, these rights apply within the technical limits of the decentralized network described above. To exercise these rights, contact us; we respond within one month.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, please contact us so we can remove it from our systems.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and take effect immediately upon posting.
