The public nature of the NOSTR protocol

NOSTR is a public-by-default protocol. Notes you publish may be received, stored, and re-published by any NOSTR relay, including after you delete them — a deletion request (NIP-09) is broadcast to the network, but relays are not obligated to honor it.

Private messages are encrypted (NIP-04 for backward compatibility with some UPlanet tools, or NIP-44/NIP-17 for modern exchanges), but some metadata (who talks to whom, and when) may remain visible depending on the scheme used. Do not share information whose confidentiality is critical.
